# CalmPaws — Content Calendar & Posting System

The goal of organic is simple: post consistently, find the hooks that pop, then double
down. You are not trying to go viral on day one. You're running enough at-bats to find a
winner, then repeating it.

## Phase 1: Prove the formula (Week 1 to 2)

**Accounts:** 1 TikTok + 1 Instagram. That's it. Do NOT spread thin yet.
**Volume:** 1 to 2 posts per day per platform. Quality of hook over quantity.
**Source:** The 30 scripts in `video-scripts.md`, rendered in Creatify.

## Phase 2: Scale what works (Week 3+)

Once one or two videos clearly outperform (saves, shares, comments, watch-through):
1. Make 2 to 3 MORE accounts per platform (Gmail + aliasing, see brand.md).
2. Remix the winning hook 10+ ways: new opening line, new B-roll, new actor, same idea.
3. Push total volume toward 20 to 40 posts/day ACROSS accounts (4 to 6 per account max).
4. Kill the angles that flop. Feed the winners.

## What "working" looks like (track these)

| Metric | Weak | Promising | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Avg view duration | under 40% | 50%+ | 65%+ |
| Saves + shares | few | climbing | high relative to views |
| Comments | quiet | questions about the product | "where do I buy this" |
| Store clicks (link in bio) | trickle | steady | spikes after a post |

Put the store link in every bio. Pin a comment with the link too.

## Posting rules that keep accounts healthy

- Upload TikTok natively from the phone, don't bulk-auto-post (ban risk).
- Space posts out (not 5 in one minute). 2 to 4 hours apart.
- Warm new accounts: first 2 to 3 days, just watch/like pet content before posting.
- Use trending sounds where they fit. Native captions on every video (Submagic later).
- Reply to comments fast, especially "where to buy." Comments boost reach.

## Instagram scheduling (mostly hands-off)

- Tool: **Metricool** (free tier) or **Postiz** (open source, free to self-host).
- Connect the CalmPaws Instagram business account.
- Batch-upload a week of Reels on Sunday, let it auto-post daily.
- TikTok stays manual/native for best reach.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Sunday (30 min):** render the week's clips in Creatify, clean in CapCut, schedule IG.
- **Daily (5 min):** post TikTok natively, reply to comments, glance at what's hitting.
- **Friday (10 min):** check metrics, note the top performer, plan next week's remixes.
