## Positioning

CalmPaws helps anxious pets feel safe, settle down, and sleep. We sell to pet parents
who treat their animals like family and will pay to stop their dog from shaking through a
storm or destroying the house with separation anxiety. We win on **empathy and story**,
not specs or price.

**One-line promise:** Help your anxious pet finally feel calm and safe.

## Voice (for store + captions + scripts)

- Talk like a real pet owner to a friend. Warm, honest, casual.
- **Never use the dash ("—" or " - ").** Periods and commas only.
- No hype, no buzzwords, no medical claims. "Helped him settle," never "cures anxiety."
- Emotional honesty over features. Pet parents buy on feeling.
