## Top 2 recommended

### 1st — PDRN / Bio-Collagen serum
Best fit for AI-UGC because it wins on **curiosity and education** ("salmon DNA
skincare?!"), exactly what a talking-head does best, with no in-hand demo needed. Rising
demand (the snail-mucin successor), fat margins, featherweight shipping, huge passionate
#SkinTok audience. The novelty gives you a 3-second hook for free.
- **Hook line:** "I stopped using snail mucin the second I found out what Korean dermatologists use instead."
- **Risk:** Topical skincare → keep claims cosmetic (hydration/glow), never medical.
  Vet supplier quality hard; people put it on their face, bad supplier = refunds + bad comments.
  Order samples before scaling.

### 2nd — Pet anxiety relief product
Different audience (good for a second content engine). Pet content has the strongest
organic spread on TikTok, the emotional hook is unbeatable ("my dog stopped shaking"),
and AI-UGC thrives on the relatable owner monologue + calm-pet B-roll. Cheap, light,
bought on emotion not price.
- **Hook line:** "If your dog hides during fireworks, stop scrolling, this fixed it for mine."
- **Risk:** Calming-bed sub-niche is crowded → lead with a fresh SKU and an honest
  emotional story, not the generic product.

## Honest overall risks (apply to all)

- **AI-UGC trust:** mid-2026 audiences spot AI actors more easily. Keep videos short,
  story-led, B-roll heavy so the AI face isn't the whole frame. Test heavily.
- **Saturation moves fast.** Every "proven" product is sold by others. The edge is the
  **angle and content**, not the product. Plan to test 3-4 SKUs, not marry one.
- **Claim risk** on skincare/wellness/red-light: stay cosmetic, never medical.
