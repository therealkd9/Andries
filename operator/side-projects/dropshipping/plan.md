# Dropshipping Store — Launch Plan

**Status:** Setup / pre-launch
**Role in the business:** Side gig. B2B AI automation stays Engine #1. This is the
"build once, feed daily" consistency play. Bias to simple and fast to launch, then
win on consistency.
**Last updated:** 2026-06-30

## The model (how this actually works)

Organic only. No paid ads. Drive traffic from short-form video to a high-converting
Shopify store.

- **Content:** Fully AI-generated UGC. AI actors talking about / reviewing the product,
  with product B-roll layered on top.
- **Volume:** NOT 30-50 posts on one account (that gets banned). The real model is
  **5 to 10 accounts per platform, 2 to 4 posts each per day** = 30-50 total, spread
  safely. Start with 3 to 5 accounts, warm them up, then scale.
- **Platforms:** TikTok (upload natively from phone for best reach) + Instagram Reels
  (can be scheduled).

### Honest limitation to design around
AI actors can talk *about* a product convincingly but cannot realistically hold and
demonstrate *your exact product* in-hand yet. So we lean into hook-driven talking-head
UGC + product B-roll, which is what converts best on organic TikTok anyway. Pick
products that sell on emotion/benefit, not on live physical demo.

## What Claude (the Operator) does vs what Andries does

**Claude can:** research and pick the product, write every hook/script/caption, build
and write the full Shopify store, design the content calendar, organize batches, set up
IG scheduling.

**Claude cannot:** reliably auto-post 30-50 videos/day across accounts (ban risk +
locked-down TikTok API), or physically render the AI videos (that's the video tool's
job, driven by Andries).

**Andries does:** run the AI video tool to render clips, upload to TikTok natively,
order product samples, approve product + store, handle the supplier/orders.

## Launch checklist

- [ ] 1. Pick the product (research in progress) ← 80% of success
- [ ] 2. Validate supplier (AliExpress / CJ Dropshipping / Zendrop) + margin check
- [ ] 3. Build Shopify store (Claude writes copy + structure; Andries sets up account)
- [ ] 4. Create 3 to 5 TikTok + IG accounts, warm them up
- [ ] 5. Write first batch of 30 video scripts/hooks (Claude)
- [ ] 6. Render first batch in Creatify (Andries)
- [ ] 7. Post 2 to 4 per account per day, track what hits
- [ ] 8. Double down on winning hooks, kill the rest

## Costs to start (kept minimal)

- Product samples: ~$15-40
- Shopify: ~$1 first month
- Creatify: free trial, then ~$33/mo (only after a product gets views)
- Everything else: free (CapCut, Postiz, account creation)
