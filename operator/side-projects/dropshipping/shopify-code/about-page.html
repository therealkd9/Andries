We started CalmPaws for the anxious ones.

Anyone who's loved an anxious pet knows the helpless feeling. The pacing. The shaking during storms. The destroyed beds and the 3am whines. We started CalmPaws because our own dogs went through it, and the "calm down" advice never helped. What helped was giving them a space that actually felt safe.

Everything we make is built around that. We're a small team obsessed with one thing: helping pets feel calm and helping their humans sleep again. If something doesn't help your pup, we don't want your money. Simple as that.
