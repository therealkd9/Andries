# Everlume — Brand Identity

**Product:** Custom 3D Photo Crystal. Upload a photo, it gets laser-etched inside a
clear crystal that glows on a small wooden LED base. A memory turned into light.
**Status:** Active build. Brand name locked as **Everlume** (swap by find-and-replace if you want).

## Name options (Everlume is the build)

| Option | Feel | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Everlume** ✅ | Warm, premium, memorable | "Ever" (forever/memory) + "lume" (glow). Says exactly what it is. |

## Positioning

Everlume turns your most important photo into a keepsake that glows. We sell to people
buying on **emotion**: someone who lost a parent, a pet, a spouse, or who wants to give a
gift that makes their mom cry in the good way. We win on feeling, not features. The
product is a frozen memory.

**One-line promise:** Keep the ones you love close, in light.

## Voice (store, captions, scripts)

- Heartfelt, sincere, a little vulnerable. Like a real person talking about someone they love.
- **Never use the dash ("—" or " - ").** Periods and commas only.
- No hype, no buzzwords, no salesy lines. The emotion sells. Let it.
- Never over-claim. This is about memory and love.

## Look / vibe (store + Creatify)

Warm and premium. Deep charcoal or midnight backgrounds so the crystal's glow pops, with
soft warm-gold light and cream text. Think "cozy, sacred, keepsake." Nothing loud or
cheap. The glowing crystal against dark is the whole aesthetic.

## Pricing
