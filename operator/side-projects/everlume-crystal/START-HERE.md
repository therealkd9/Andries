## ⚡ Do this when you're back (about 45 min, then post)

### 1. Make 2 accounts (5 min)
One TikTok, one Instagram. Handle `@everlume` or a variant from `brand.md`. Use the Gmail
aliases in `brand.md`. Put your store link in both bios (add the link once the store is live).

### 2. Stand up the store (25 min) — follow `shopify-setup-guide.md`
Fast path:
1. shopify.com → free trial → name it Everlume.
2. Add the product. Title + paste `shopify-code/product-description.html`. Price 49.95 / compare 89.95. Add Size variants.
3. Dawn theme → Customize → Add section → **Custom Liquid** → paste `shopify-code/homepage-custom-liquid.html`.
4. Add About + FAQ pages (paste the two HTML files).
5. Generate policies. Add the email popup + Judge.me reviews (seed reviews are in `store-copy.md`).
6. Remove the password page to go live. You can launch on the free myshopify URL tonight and add a real domain later.

### 3. Set up the photo flow (2 min)
Since it's personalized, tell buyers to reply to their order email with their photo (see
Step 3 in the setup guide). Good enough for launch night.

### 4. Make your first videos (10 min) — Creatify
1. Go to creatify.ai, start the free trial.
2. Open `video-scripts.md`. Paste **Script 1** into Creatify, pick an AI actor, generate.
3. Do 2 or 3 more (Scripts 2, 3, 4). Lightly clean in CapCut (free) if needed. Add captions.

### 5. Post (now)
- Upload to TikTok **natively from your phone**. Schedule the others.
- 1 to 3 posts today. Link in bio. Pin a comment with your store link.
