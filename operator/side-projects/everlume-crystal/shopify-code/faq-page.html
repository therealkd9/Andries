Frequently asked questions

What photo works best?

A clear, well-lit photo with the faces easy to see. You can upload it right after ordering, and we'll clean it up for the engraving.

How long does it take?

Each crystal is made to order and engraved by hand, so allow a few days to craft it, plus shipping. We'll keep you updated the whole way.

Is the light base included?

Yes, every crystal comes with its wooden LED base.

Can I send it as a gift?

Absolutely. A lot of our orders are gifts. Add a note at checkout and we'll make it special.

What if something's wrong with it?

We hand-check every crystal before it ships, and you're covered by our 30-day happiness guarantee. If it's not right, we make it right or refund you.
