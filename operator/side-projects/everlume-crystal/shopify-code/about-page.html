Everlume started with one photo.

We started Everlume because a photo on a phone screen never felt like enough. When you lose someone, or a moment matters more than words, you want to hold it. To see it. To light it up in the dark and feel them close.

So we make keepsakes out of light. We take the photo that matters most to you and etch it inside crystal, by hand, checked by real people who understand it's not just a product, it's a memory. If it's not right, we make it right. Every time. Because you only get one shot at this kind of gift, and it has to be perfect.
