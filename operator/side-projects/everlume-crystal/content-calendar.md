# Everlume — Content Calendar & Posting System

Organic wins on consistency. You're not trying to go viral tonight. You're running enough
at-bats to find the story angles that hit, then repeating them.

## Phase 1: Prove the formula (Week 1 to 2)

**Accounts:** 1 TikTok + 1 Instagram. Do NOT spread thin yet.
**Volume:** 1 to 3 posts per day per platform. Emotion over polish.
**Source:** the 30 scripts in `video-scripts.md`, rendered in Creatify.

Work through all 30. **Post TikTok natively from your phone.** Schedule Instagram.

## Phase 2: Scale what works (Week 3+)

When one or two videos clearly outperform (saves, shares, "where do I get this" comments):
1. Add 2 to 3 more accounts per platform (Gmail + aliasing, see `brand.md`).
2. Remix the winning story 10+ ways: new opening line, new B-roll, new AI actor, same emotion.
3. Push total volume toward 20 to 40 posts/day ACROSS accounts (4 to 6 per account max).
4. Kill the flops. Feed the winners.

## What "working" looks like

| Metric | Weak | Promising | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Avg view duration | under 40% | 50%+ | 65%+ |
| Saves + shares | few | climbing | high vs views (this product gets SHARED, it's emotional) |
| Comments | quiet | "this made me cry" | "where do I buy this / link?" |
| Store clicks (link in bio) | trickle | steady | spikes after a post |

Link in every bio. Pin a comment with the link on every video.

## Account-health rules

- Upload TikTok natively from the phone. Don't bulk auto-post (ban risk).
- Space posts 2 to 4 hours apart. Not 5 in one minute.
- Warm new accounts: first 2 to 3 days just watch/like emotional + gift content before posting.
- Use soft, emotional trending sounds. Captions on every video.
- Reply to comments fast, especially "link?" Comments boost reach.

## This product's edge: it gets SHARED

Everlume content is emotional, which means people tag friends ("this is what we should get
mom") and share to stories. That sharing is free reach. Lean into tearjerker storytelling
and gift angles, that's what spreads.

## Instagram scheduling (hands-off)

- Tool: **Metricool** (free) or **Postiz** (open source, free to self-host).
- Batch-upload a week of Reels, let it auto-post. TikTok stays native.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Sunday (30 min):** render the week's clips in Creatify, clean in CapCut, schedule IG.
- **Daily (5 min):** post TikTok natively, reply to comments, note what's hitting.
- **Friday (10 min):** check metrics, note the top performer, plan next week's remixes.
