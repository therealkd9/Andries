# Everlume | 30 AI-UGC Video Scripts

Heartfelt talking-head stories for the glowing 3D photo crystal. An AI actor plays a real person telling a personal story to camera while B-roll of the crystal glows. The actor never demonstrates anything. The emotion does the selling.

### Script 1: The First Christmas Without Him

So I took my favorite photo of us, the one from my graduation where he's laughing, and I had it turned into this little crystal that lights up. - **B-roll notes:** Dark living room lit only by the crystal, the graduation photo visible etched inside, a hand slowly adjusting it on the mantel, warm glow reflecting on a face with soft eyes.
