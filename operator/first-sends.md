## Reality check on channel
Only 2 of these 5 have a public email (Fontenot's, Hibbard). Small home-service
shops often don't publish one. For the rest, the play is: text the business line,
DM their Facebook, use their website contact form, or have the researcher dig up an
email. The message works on any channel.
