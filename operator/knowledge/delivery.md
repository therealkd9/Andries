# Delivery Playbook — How You Actually Fulfill a "Yes"

The front half (find, demo, pitch) means nothing if you can't deliver. Good news:
you do not build this from scratch. There's a platform built for exactly this.

## The engine: HighLevel (GoHighLevel)

HighLevel is the tool automation agencies use to deliver "missed call text back +
AI booking + CRM + reviews" for local businesses. It does 90% of your offer out of
the box. You resell it as your own service.

- **Missed call text back:** built in. A call comes in, nobody answers, the system
  instantly texts the caller back. This IS your core demo, made real.
- **AI conversation / booking:** the "AI Employee" add-on answers, qualifies, and
  books the appointment over text (and even voice). This is the smart part of the demo.
- **CRM + calendar + review requests:** all included.
- **Sub-accounts:** you get one agency account and spin up a separate sub-account
  per client. Build it once, clone it for every new client.

## From "yes" to live — the client setup SOP

When a business says yes, this is the repeatable process (a few hours per client):
1. **Collect:** their business name, phone number, service area, hours, main
   services, and how they want appointments booked.
2. **Create a sub-account** in HighLevel for them.
3. **Get them a number:** either a new HighLevel/Twilio number, or forward their
   existing business line so missed calls trigger the text-back.
4. **Turn on missed-call-text-back** with a message in their voice (use the demo
   script as the template).
5. **Set up the AI booking flow** (AI Employee): the questions to ask, the calendar
   to book into, what counts as a qualified lead.
6. **Connect their calendar** (or a HighLevel calendar) so booked jobs land somewhere.
7. **Add review requests** after a job (easy extra win to show value fast).
8. **Test it:** call their number, don't answer, confirm the text fires and books.
9. **Hand off a simple dashboard** so the owner sees leads caught. Bill the monthly.

Clone that sub-account setup for every future client. It gets faster each time.

  natural responses** via webhooks/API when a client wants something custom.
- Use Claude to **write each client's text scripts, qualifying questions, and

## The cheaper, more technical alternative (later)
Twilio (numbers + SMS) + Make/n8n (glue) + Claude (the brain) + Google Calendar.
More control, lower per-client cost, much more setup. Revisit once you have a few
clients and want fatter margins. Start with HighLevel for speed to first dollar.
