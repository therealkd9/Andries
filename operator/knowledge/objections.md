# Objections & Responses — v1

Stay calm, agree, reframe to the outcome. Never argue. The goal is the next step,
not winning the point.

**"How much is it?"**
> Setup is a one-time {$1,500–3,500} depending on how much you want automated, then
> {$500–1,000}/month to run and maintain it. But the real question is what one
> extra booked job a week is worth to you — this usually pays for itself fast.

**"I already have someone answer the phone."**
> Love that — this is for the calls they *miss*: after hours, on jobs, double-booked.
> It only fires when no one picks up, so you catch the leads slipping through.

**"I'm too busy / not interested right now."**
> Totally fair — that busyness is exactly the problem this solves. Can I send a
> 2-minute video so it's there whenever you've got a slow moment? No call needed.

**"Does this really work / sounds too good."**
> Fair to be skeptical. Let me just show you — I'll set up a live demo with your
> business name on it so you see exactly what a customer would get. If it's not
> obviously useful, no worries.

**"I don't have time to learn new software."**
> You won't touch it. I build it, I run it, it works in the background. You just
> get more booked jobs and a simple summary. That's the whole point.

**"Let me think about it."**
> Of course. What's the one thing you'd want to be sure of before moving forward?
> (Then handle that specific thing.) Want me to hold a spot to set it up this week?

**"Can you guarantee results?"**
> Here's my version of a guarantee: {pilot offer — e.g., "I'll set it up, and if it
> doesn't catch leads you were missing in the first 30 days, you don't pay the
> setup."} I'd rather earn the monthly than argue about the setup.

## The de-risk play (for early clients)
You have no case studies yet. Trade proof for price/risk-reversal:
- Offer a **discounted or pilot setup** for the first 2–3 clients in exchange for a
  testimonial + permission to use results.
- Frame it honestly: "You'd be one of my first {trade} clients, so I'll over-deliver
  and make the setup easy on you in exchange for a review if you love it."
Early clients buy the founder. Sell the hustle and the outcome.
