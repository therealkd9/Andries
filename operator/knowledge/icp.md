# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — v1

The Operator uses this to qualify leads before spending time on them. Tune as you
learn who actually buys.

## Fits (go after these)
- **Industry:** home services — HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, landscaping,
  garage doors, pest control, water damage/restoration, pool service.
- **Size:** $500k–$5M/yr revenue; ~3–25 employees. Big enough to afford it, small
  enough that the owner decides fast.
- **Structure:** owner-operated or owner is still close to operations.
- **Tells of pain (the good signs):**
  - Slow to answer the phone / goes to voicemail (test it — call them).
  - No instant reply when you fill out their website form.
  - Lots of reviews mentioning "never called back" / "hard to reach."
  - Running ads (Google/Facebook) — they're paying for leads they may be dropping.
  - Active but messy online presence — they care about growth but lack systems.

## Red flags (skip or deprioritize)
- Huge franchises / national chains (slow, committee decisions).
- Businesses with zero online presence and no website (hard to help, low budget).
- Pre-revenue or clearly struggling to stay open (can't pay).
- Industries needing heavy compliance you don't understand yet (medical, legal) —
  revisit once you have wins.

## How to find them
- Google Maps / local search: "[trade] near [city]" → scrape name, phone, site,
  review count, rating.
- Facebook/Instagram business pages in the niche.
- Ad libraries (Facebook Ad Library, Google) — businesses running ads = paying for
  leads = strong fit.
- Local directories, Yelp, Nextdoor.

## What to capture per lead (for pipeline.md)
Business name · owner name (if findable) · trade · city · phone · website ·
email/DM handle · a one-line "pain hook" (the specific reason they're a fit, e.g.
"didn't answer test call at 2pm" or "running FB ads since March").

## The qualification gut-check
Before outreach, the Operator should be able to answer:
**"What specific money is this business losing that we can visibly fix?"**
If there's no clear answer, it's not a lead yet.
