# Outreach Templates — v1

Principles: short, specific, about THEM, one clear ask (a quick call or a "want me
to show you?"). Personalize the first line with a real detail. Never send a wall of
text. Always make the value concrete (lost jobs, saved hours, booked appointments).

The `{curly}` bits get filled per lead by the Operator.

---

## Cold Email

**Subject lines (test a few):**
- `quick question about {business} leads`
- `{city} {trade} — missing calls?`
- `caught something on your site`

**Body — v1 (problem-led):**
```
Hi {ownerName},

I called {business} earlier as a test and {it went to voicemail / it took a while
to reach anyone} — happens to most {trade} shops when you're out on jobs, but each
missed call can be a $300–$3,000 job gone to whoever picked up first.

I set up an AI system that instantly texts back missed calls, answers the basics,
and books the appointment — so you stop losing those leads even when you can't
get to the phone.

Worth a 10-minute look at what it'd catch for you? I can show you a quick example
built for {trade}.

— Andries
```

**Follow-up 1 (2–3 days later):**
```
Hey {ownerName} — following up. Quick math: if {business} misses even 5 calls a
week and books half, that's a few thousand dollars a month walking out the door.
The fix takes me a few days to set up. Want me to send a 2-min video of how it works?
```

**Follow-up 2 (4–5 days after that — the breakup):**
```
{ownerName}, I'll stop here so I'm not a pest. If catching more of your inbound
leads is ever a priority, just reply "lead" and I'll send the example. Either way,
wishing {business} a strong season.
```

## Instagram / Facebook DM

**Opener:**
```
Hey {ownerName} — are you guys able to catch every call that comes in during busy
days? Asking because I help {trade} businesses auto-text-back missed calls so
they stop losing jobs. Mind if I send a quick example?
```

**If they reply:**
```
Awesome. Here's the gist: someone calls and you can't pick up → they instantly get
a text from "{business}" that answers, qualifies, and books them. Most shops are
losing 20–40% of calls. Want me to set up a quick call to show you what it'd look
like for you?
```

## Phone / Voicemail script (high-conversion, uncomfortable, worth it)

**Live:**
```
Hi, is this {ownerName}? — I'll be quick. I called as a test a bit ago and it went
to voicemail. I build a system for {trade} businesses that instantly texts back
missed calls and books the appointment, so you stop losing jobs when you're on
site. Can I show you what it'd catch for you in about 10 minutes this week?
```

**Voicemail:**
```
Hey {ownerName}, it's Andries. I help {trade} shops stop losing jobs from missed
calls — I noticed yours went to voicemail earlier. Quick idea that could book you
a few more jobs a week. I'll text you the details. Thanks!
```

---

## The call goal
Every channel has ONE job: **book a 15–20 min call** (or get a clear "send me the
example"). Don't try to close in the DM. Get the conversation.

## After a "yes, show me"
- Confirm the call time; add to pipeline with the date.
- Prep: pull their reviews, test-call them, note 1–2 specific things you'd fix.
- Bring a concrete "here's what I'd set up for {business}" — specificity closes.
