# Outreach Templates — v1

Principles: short, specific, about THEM, one clear ask (a quick call or a "want me
to show you?"). Personalize the first line with a real detail. Never send a wall of
text. Always make the value concrete (lost jobs, saved hours, booked appointments).

The `{curly}` bits get filled per lead by the Operator.

## Voice rules (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
- **Never use the dash ("—" or " - ") in any message.** It's a dead AI giveaway.
  Use periods, commas, or just two short sentences instead.
- **Write like a real person, with proper capitalization.** It's a business email,
  not a text to a friend. Casual and human, contractions are good, but capitalize
  normally. Short. One thought per line.
- **Hook first, then the link, fast.** The job of the top two lines is the click.
  Everything under the link is only for the ones already curious.
- No corporate buzzwords, no "I hope this email finds you well," no hype, no emojis
  spammed everywhere (one is plenty).
- Read it out loud. If it sounds like a human friend, it's right. If it sounds like
  ChatGPT, rewrite it.

## ⭐ Demo-led outreach (PRIMARY approach)

A plain cold message gets ignored. Lead with a **personalized live demo** instead —
a phone-mockup page showing THEIR business auto-answering and booking a customer.
It's a gift they want to open, not a pitch.

**Build the link per prospect** (hosted on Pages):
```
https://therealkd9.github.io/Andries/demo/?name={Business Name}&type={trade}
```
`type` = hvac · plumbing · roofing · electrical · landscaping · garage · pest
(URL-encode the name: spaces → %20, & → %26, / → %2F)

**The email (the "lost customer" hook — primary template):**

Open as if you're a frustrated customer they missed, then reveal it fast. The
reveal IS the pitch. Swap the trade-specific problem line per business.

```
Subject: I called {Business} and never heard back

Hey, I called a few days ago when {trade problem, e.g. "my AC went out" /
"my pipe was leaking"} but never got a call back, and I needed someone quicker
than I'd hoped.

Okay, full honesty: I'm not actually a customer. But someone exactly like me
probably did call this week, and you never even knew you lost them.

That's why I built you this. It's a 30 second demo of what your customers could
get the second they call and the crew can't pick up: {link}

It answers them, gets the job details, and books the appointment automatically.
So the next "me" turns into a booked job instead of a missed call.

If it looks useful, I'll set it up for {Business}. Worth a quick reply?

Andries
```

**Follow-up (2 to 3 days later):**
```
No worries if you're slammed. Quick math though, even 5 missed calls a week at a
few hundred each adds up fast. Happy to tailor the demo to exactly how {Business}
runs. Want me to?
```

Goal of the demo link: a reply / a booked call. Everything below is backup for when
a demo isn't a fit.

## Cold Email

**Subject lines (test a few):**
- `quick question about {business} leads`
- `{city} {trade} — missing calls?`
- `caught something on your site`

**Body — v1 (problem-led):**
```
Hi {ownerName},

I called {business} earlier as a test and {it went to voicemail / it took a while
to reach anyone} — happens to most {trade} shops when you're out on jobs, but each
missed call can be a $300–$3,000 job gone to whoever picked up first.

I set up an AI system that instantly texts back missed calls, answers the basics,
and books the appointment — so you stop losing those leads even when you can't
get to the phone.

Worth a 10-minute look at what it'd catch for you? I can show you a quick example
built for {trade}.

— Andries
```

**Follow-up 1 (2–3 days later):**
```
Hey {ownerName} — following up. Quick math: if {business} misses even 5 calls a
week and books half, that's a few thousand dollars a month walking out the door.
The fix takes me a few days to set up. Want me to send a 2-min video of how it works?
```

**Follow-up 2 (4–5 days after that — the breakup):**
```
{ownerName}, I'll stop here so I'm not a pest. If catching more of your inbound
leads is ever a priority, just reply "lead" and I'll send the example. Either way,
wishing {business} a strong season.
```

## The call goal
Every channel has ONE job: **book a 15–20 min call** (or get a clear "send me the
example"). Don't try to close in the DM. Get the conversation.

## After a "yes, show me"
- Confirm the call time; add to pipeline with the date.
- Prep: pull their reviews, test-call them, note 1–2 specific things you'd fix.
- Bring a concrete "here's what I'd set up for {business}" — specificity closes.
