## The shape of the offer

**Who:** Local service businesses that live or die on inbound leads but are bad at
responding fast — **home services first** (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical,
landscaping, garage doors, pest control). Owner-operated, $500k–$5M/yr, reachable,
feel the pain in dollars.

*Why this niche:* they bleed money on missed calls and slow follow-up, the ROI of
fixing it is obvious and immediate, owners make fast decisions, and there are tens
of thousands of them. It's the most beginner-friendly path to fast "yes"es.

**What (the productized service): "Never Miss a Lead" — an AI back-office system that:**
- **Instantly responds** to every new lead (missed call → text back in seconds,
  form fills, DMs) so they stop losing jobs to whoever answers first.
- **Qualifies & books** — an AI assistant (Claude-powered) asks the right
  questions, captures job details, and pushes the lead toward a booked appointment.
- **Follows up automatically** — multi-touch nudges so warm leads don't go cold.
- **Logs everything** into their CRM/spreadsheet + a simple owner dashboard.
- **Asks for reviews** after the job, automatically.

Built on glue tools (Make/Zapier/n8n) + Claude for the smart parts. You're selling
**booked jobs and saved hours**, not "automation."

**The pitch in one line:**
> "I build an AI system that instantly answers and follows up with every new lead
> so you stop losing jobs — most [plumbers/roofers] are missing 20–40% of their
> calls. Want me to show you what it'd catch for you?"

## Pricing (designed for fast cash + recurring/exitable MRR)

| Tier | Setup (one-time) | Monthly (recurring) |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $1,500 | $500/mo |
| **Pro** (recommended) | $2,500 | $750/mo |
| **Done-for-you** | $3,500 | $1,000/mo |

- **Setup fees** give you quick cash to fund momentum and pay Dad back.
- **Monthly retainers** are the real prize — recurring, and what makes Andries
  eventually **exitable**. Optimize for getting clients onto monthly.

## The math to $8–10k/month
Recurring-only target with **Pro retainers ($750/mo)**:
- **~12 retainer clients = ~$9,000/mo recurring.** That's the 90-day build.
- Plus setup fees along the way: each new Pro client = **$2,500 cash up front**.
- Faster bridge: **4 clients in month 1** = $10,000 in setup fees + $3,000/mo
  starting to stack. Setup fees get you to "$8–10k earned" quickly; retainers get
  you to "$8–10k recurring" by ~Sep 30.

Pick the framing that fits: **earned** $8–10k (setup-fee heavy, faster) vs.
**recurring** $8–10k MRR (retainer-heavy, the durable version). Recommended: chase
both — lead with setup cash, anchor on the retainer.

## Why they say yes
- The pain is **measured in lost jobs** ($300–$5,000 each). One saved job pays for
  months of service.
- You're **fast and personal** — a real person who'll set it up for them, not
  faceless software.
- **Low risk** — offer a pilot/guarantee early (see objections.md) to remove fear.
