# Growing the Operator — Daily Skill-Building

The vision: an employee you check in with every day that does more and more on its
own, until it runs the whole chain — from finding businesses to collecting cash —
with you barely touching it.

This file is how we get there. It's the Operator's training plan and its memory.

## How "leveling up" works
The Operator gets more capable when we upgrade its **brain** (playbooks, knowledge,
tools, memory) and expand what it's allowed to do on its own. Each day we advance
one rung. The repo is the brain; it compounds.

## Autonomy levels
- 🔴 **Andries does it** — the Operator can't do this yet.
- 🟡 **Drafts → Andries approves** — the Operator does the work; Andries hits "go."
- 🟢 **Autonomous** — the Operator does it without asking.

## The daily check-in ("Operator Standup") — ~10 min
Do this with the Operator every day:
1. **Results** — what happened since last time? (replies, calls, $ in) Log it below.
2. **Learn** — what worked / what flopped? Update the relevant playbook.
3. **Advance** — pick **one** rung and push it one level toward 🟢. Do the work to
   enable that (write the playbook, add the tool, grant the permission).
4. **Today** — the 1–3 actions that move a real prospect closer to "yes."

The reminder app already nudges you. This is what you do when it does.
